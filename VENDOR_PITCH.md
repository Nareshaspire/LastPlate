# LastPlate 🌿 — 5-Minute Vendor Pitch & Partner Playbook

A structured, conversational pitch script tailored for local bakery owners, cafe managers, restaurants, and grocers to turn end-of-day surplus into recovered margin and new foot traffic.

---

## 🎙️ The 5-Minute Vendor Pitch Script

**Target Audience:** Restaurant owners, bakery managers, cafe operators, grocery managers  
**Live Platform Demo:** [lastplate-production.up.railway.app](https://lastplate-production.up.railway.app/)

---

### Minute 0:00 – 1:00: The Hook & Shared Pain

> "Hi **[Owner/Manager Name]**, thanks for giving me two minutes. First off, your food display looks incredible. 
>
> Quick question: tonight at closing time around 8 or 9 PM, when the doors lock, what happens to the 5 croissants, 3 artisan sandwiches, or prepped boxes that didn’t sell today?
>
> In almost every neighborhood spot I speak with, the answer is the same: you either compost it, let staff take a fraction, or it goes straight into a black garbage bag. 
> 
> You’ve already paid for the ingredients, packaging, and labor to make it. Throwing it out hurts financially and emotionally. What if you could recover **$30 to $80 cash every single evening** on items you were about to discard, with zero extra prep work?"

---

### Minute 1:00 – 2:00: The Math & The Opportunity

> "That’s why we built **LastPlate**. 
>
> LastPlate is a local marketplace connecting nearby residents with your end-of-day surplus. We never ask you to discount your regular menu during normal business hours—your lunch rush still pays full price.
>
> Instead, 30 minutes before closing, you group whatever is left into a **'Surprise Bag'** or **'LastPlate Bundle'** at a 50% to 70% discount. 
> 
> Let's look at the simple math:
> - Sell just **4 surplus bags an evening at $7 each** = **$28/day**.
> - In a month, that is **$840 in recovered bottom-line profit**.
> - Over a year, that’s over **$10,000 back into your business** from food that was destined for the dumpster. That covers your electricity bill or an employee's monthly payroll."

---

### Minute 2:00 – 3:00: Zero Extra Work (The 30-Second Workflow)

> "The #1 question owners ask is: *'I’m already exhausted at closing time—do I have time to list individual items?'*
>
> The answer is **no**. You don't list individual items. Here is how simple it is:
>
> 1. **Take 30 seconds at 7:30 PM**: Open your seller tab, type *'3 mixed muffins and 2 sourdough baguettes'*.
> 2. **Our built-in AI auto-generates the listing**: It sets the price, tags, and pickup window automatically. You hit *Publish*.
> 3. **Shoppers pre-pay online**: They arrive at your counter during your designated 30-minute pickup window, show you a 4-digit code on their phone, you hand them the bag, and you're done. 
> 
> No delivery drivers. No tablets beeping all day. No 30% delivery cuts."

---

### Minute 3:00 – 4:00: Foot Traffic & New Regular Customers

> "Here is the biggest hidden benefit: **New foot traffic.**
>
> When customers walk through your front door to pick up their LastPlate bag:
> - **42% of them buy something else on impulse**—a hot latte, a bottle of juice, or a specialty dessert from your display fridge.
> - They discover your shop for the first time. The student or young professional who rescues a $6 surprise box on a Tuesday night is the exact customer who comes back on Saturday morning to buy a full-priced brunch with their friends.
> - And your brand is highlighted in the community as an eco-conscious, zero-waste business that cares about the neighborhood."

---

### Minute 4:00 – 5:00: The Zero-Risk Pilot & Close

> "There are **zero upfront fees, zero setup costs, and no contract.** 
>
> If you have surplus tonight, you make money. If you sell out completely on your own, you list nothing and pay nothing. You have total control.
>
> We are onboarding a hand-picked group of 10 local culinary spots for our initial launch in the area. I’d love to feature **[Business Name]** as one of our flagship partners.
>
> Can we run a simple 3-day test starting this Thursday? It takes 60 seconds to create your merchant profile right now."

---

## 🛡️ Quick Answers to Common Vendor Objections

| Vendor Concern | Your Confident Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Will this cannibalize my regular full-price customers?"** | *"Not at all. Pickups only happen in the final 30–60 minutes before closing, and items are packaged as surprise bags. Regular daytime customers who need specific items for lunch or dinner will never wait until 8:45 PM for an unpredictable leftover bag."* |
| **"What if on some days I have zero food left over?"** | *"That’s great news! You only create a listing on days you actually have surplus. If you sell out at 5 PM, you do nothing. You have 100% control every day."* |
| **"Do we have to deliver the food?"** | *"Never. LastPlate is 100% customer pickup. Customers walk into your shop to pick up their order, which saves you delivery fees and brings physical foot traffic."* |
| **"What packaging do we need?"** | *"Whatever paper bags or boxes you already use for takeaway orders. No special packaging required."* |
